# 🎮 Minigame

- The executor must have operator (OP) permissions enabled.

## 👥 Multi

### 🏆 AdvRace

- **AdvRace is a competitive mini-game where players race to complete as many Minecraft advancements as possible within a limited time.
Each advancement grants points, and the player or team with the highest total score at the end wins.**
- [Optional features] Bonus points for being the first player to complete an advancement / Random score multipliers assigned to specific advancements each game / Increased points for high-difficulty advancements / Team-based gameplay support / etc...
- https://github.com/sam-ple/minescript-memo/blob/sample/02_minigame/11_advrace/advrace2_v0.0.05_20251225.py


### ⛏️ OreRace

- **OreRace is a mining-focused competition where players collect ores within a time limit.
Each type of ore has a predefined point value, and the player or team with the highest score wins.**
- [Optional features] Temporary double-point events / Team-based gameplay support / etc...
- https://github.com/sam-ple/minescript-memo/blob/sample/02_minigame/15_orerace/orerace_v0.0.09_20251226.py

### 🏃 LineRace

- **LineRace challenges players to run along a designated lane of blocks and see how far they can progress within a time limit.
If a player leaves the lane, they are instantly returned to the starting point.
The player who reaches the farthest distance before time runs out wins.**
- https://github.com/sam-ple/minescript-memo/blob/sample/02_minigame/18_linerace/linerace_v0.0.09_20251225.py

### 🌍 BiomeRace

- **BiomeRace begins with a short exploration phase in Creative Mode, allowing players to freely scout the world.
After this phase, players switch to the main challenge: visiting as many different biomes as possible within a set time limit.
The player or team that visits the most unique biomes wins.**
- https://github.com/sam-ple/minescript-memo/blob/sample/02_minigame/22_biomerace/biomerace_v0.0.01_20251222.py

### 🎒 ItemRace

- **ItemRace is a time-attack mini-game focused on obtaining specific target items.
Players are detected and scored when they sneak while holding the required item.
The fastest player to complete the objective wins.**
- https://github.com/sam-ple/minescript-memo/blob/sample/02_minigame/23_itemrace/itemrace_v0.0.06_20251226.py

### 🎲 PeakOrPain

- **Peak or Pain is a roulette-style luck-based mini-game.
A random player—or sometimes all players—receives a randomly selected effect, which can be either beneficial or harmful.
Heaven or hell… it all depends on luck.**
- https://github.com/sam-ple/minescript-memo/blob/sample/02_minigame/12_peakorpain/peakorpain_v0.0.06_20251009.py

### 🛠️ CraftRace

- **CraftRace is a competitive mini-game where players compete by crafting as many items as possible within a limited time.
  The player or team with the highest number of crafted items wins.**

### ⌛ 10Minutes

- **Can you complete each objective within 10 minutes?**
  - **Achieve 15 advancements.**
  - **Obtain full iron equipment (full iron armor).**
  - **Build a Nether portal and enter the Nether.**
  - **Fill your inventory entirely with different items (no duplicates).**
- https://github.com/sam-ple/minescript-memo/blob/sample/02_minigame/25_10min/10min_v0.0.02_20260107.py
